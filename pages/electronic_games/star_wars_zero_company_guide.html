Section 02

Playing the Characters

What each Operator should actually do once a mission starts.

Hawks

Open with Combat Recon on grouped enemies to generate Advantage. Stay mid-range, maintain sight lines, use Fearless Leader / Combat Stim on key attackers, then spend spare AP on rifle shots or Overwatch.

Support / Advantage

Trick

Move into the front line early. Use Bulwark / taunt effects to pull enemy fire onto him, then let Unstoppable punish attackers. Keep him between threats and your squishier Operators.

Tank / Frontline

Cly

Get reasonably close to grouped enemies. Apply ElectroWeb Dart to several targets, reposition with Combat Jump when useful, then trigger ElectroWeb Surge. Finish weakened targets with pistol fire.

AoE / Control

Jae

Find a safe long sight line and avoid needless movement. Use Zero In early, then shoot or Overwatch priority targets. Let allies soften enemies so Final Shot can finish them automatically.

Sniper / Finisher

Luco

Set up at long range immediately. Activate Combat Gas early, use Zero In, then stay still wherever possible. Build strong Overwatch lanes and delete exposed or high-value targets.

Sniper / Overwatch

Kabb

Close distance quickly. Use Uppercut when you can secure a kill and refund AP. Use Bull Rush / Uppercut to knock enemies from cover or off ledges. If enemies bunch up, switch to Sweeping Salvo.

Melee / Disruption

Tel-Rea

Play her as a battlefield controller first, melee fighter second. Use Push / Pull to remove enemies from cover or shields, push them off ledges, or feed them into Overwatch. Move in with the lightsaber once targets are isolated.

Force Control / Melee

Astromech

Keep it behind the main squad. Scan groups early, use Built-In Comlink to give AP to the Operator who can make the biggest play, and save Coordinated Support for major turns.

AP / Utility

Medic Recruit

Turn 1: Combat Stim your biggest damage dealer — often Cly, Kabb, Jae or Luco. Stay behind Trick/Kabb, keep healing range, use Medpac only for meaningful damage, and otherwise shoot, Overwatch or Call for Backup. Save Morale Boost for when several allies need sustain.

Healing / Buffing
Front line: Trick, Kabb and Tel-Rea manipulate space, attention and enemy positioning.
Damage: Cly exploits groups; Jae and Luco punish from range.
Enablers: Hawks, Medic and Astromech increase the effectiveness of everyone else.

Upgrades

Facility, crew and weapon upgrade priorities, followed by the recommended overall purchase sequence.

Facilities

PriorityUpgradeWhy
1Black MarketOpens Weapon Mods + Utilities. Highest early value.
2Black Market Quality IBetter-quality gear appears in the shop.
3Medbay IMore recovery capacity; protects your core squad from downtime.
4NetworkingMore Influence, accelerating useful regional rewards.
5Droid Repair BayMove this higher if you want an Astromech in your main rotation.
6LoungeUseful once unlocked through Teamwork; benched Operators continue gaining Bond XP.
7Crew QuartersUseful, but a strong core roster matters more than simply having more recruits.
8Further Black Market QualityImprove once your Credit income becomes comfortable.
9Further MedbayIncrease priority on harder difficulties or if you're accumulating Injuries.
Rule of thumb: Black Market first is the strongest general early-game value. Medbay vs Droid Repair Bay depends on whether you are leaning into organic Operators or Astromechs.

Crew Upgrades

PriorityUpgradeWhy
1Combat ReadinessExtra starting Advantage; directly feeds Hawks, Cly and Tel-Rea abilities.
2Additional Weapon Mod SlotExcellent roster-wide improvement.
3Teamwork → IIIUnlocks the Lounge and improves Bond development.
4Utility Items → VEventually gives everyone another Utility slot. Very strong.
5Crew FocusMore Focus / progression value across the company.
6Remaining Teamwork / utility improvementsGood, but less urgent than the upgrades above.
Flex point: If Teamwork III becomes available before some of the other options, moving it up is reasonable because the Lounge generates long-term Bond value even for benched Operators.

Weapon spending: A weapon upgrade helps only characters using that weapon family, so upgrade around your actual field team.
